## Capacity and cleanup

The endpoint was configured for one GPU per worker, minimum zero and maximum one.
Runpod nevertheless listed one running worker and two initializing workers. All
three jobs executed on the same running worker. This discrepancy was observed
only after the terminal round-2 result; the endpoint was immediately deleted after
collecting the remaining result. No replacement jobs or further allocation were
started. A fresh list confirmed its absence, both historical endpoints at zero,
and zero standalone pods. The 29-minute deletion watchdog was terminated after
verified early cleanup.

The announced serverless rate was $1.10/GPU-hour. Reported execution time totals
about 61.575 seconds (approximately $0.0188 at that rate); this is **not the bill**
and excludes startup, idle, initializing workers and storage. Before more paid
validation, use a controllable single-GPU allocation or resolve the provider's
capacity discrepancy. Do not infer that max=1 alone established a one-worker cap.
